About Me

Interior concepts shaped through detail, material, and light.

I am Abhishek Thumbar, an interior designer and 3D artist focused on elegant residential interiors and realistic visualization. My work brings together 3D modeling, rendering, space planning, mood development, and material selection to help clients understand a space before it is built.

Each project is approached with a balance of function and atmosphere: clean layouts, warm finishes, practical storage, curated lighting, and a visual language that feels polished without feeling crowded.
